# Bunny World 0.2.0.30 — Government interface

UI-only addition over .29: two simple portrait silhouettes, a centered 100-seat neutral parliament and five clickable government-property menus. Open through Government in the top toolbar or Open government in your country panel.

Properties start unset and can be changed or cleared immediately. Choices persist while reopening the panel during the session; new campaigns and loads clear them. No historical leadership, party seats, elections, political costs, country data or save fields are added. Geography/display stay .29; no new binary assets are needed.

Pull main, run Prepare_BunnyWorld.bat, open in Godot 4.5.1 Standard and start a fresh campaign. See TEST_RESULTS.md for checks and the short test order. No Windows EXE is included.
