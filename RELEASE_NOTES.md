# Bunny World 0.2.1 — Compact politics and political power

Government is narrower, with clickable current-property cards opening a small selection window. Each actual change costs 100 political power. The action rechecks affordability before charging; unchanged, invalid and foreign-country requests are rejected.

Country control and diplomacy share small portrait placeholders and a centered neutral parliament above smaller 3-by-2 stats. Political power is removed from these stats. Only your own parliament links to Government.

Every completed simulation day grants +1 PP. The top-bar hover shows total daily gain and named sources, colored green/red by sign. No fabricated government modifiers are applied. Properties, balance and any active named modifiers persist in save schema 18; start a fresh campaign. No older-save retrofit.

Pull main, run Prepare_BunnyWorld.bat, then open in Godot 4.5.1 Standard. Geography/display remain .29. No map rebuild, extra binary assets or executable export is included.
