Natural Earth map units and populated-place coordinates
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector

Natural Earth data is public domain. No permission is needed to use it.
The bundled inputs retain geographic coordinate values and selected properties;
unused descriptive metadata was removed. The game's scenario ownership and
population come from the supplied Millennium Dawn: Bunny scenario, not Natural
Earth's contemporary demographic estimates.

Layers: ne_10m_admin_0_map_units; ne_10m_populated_places_simple; ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.
