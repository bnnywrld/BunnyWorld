BunnyWorld 0.1.6 geographic inputs and derived data (including 0.1.5 control seed)

GSHHG 2.3.7 high-resolution shorelines and lakes
Paul Wessel (University of Hawaii) and Walter H. F. Smith (NOAA), with contributors.
https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/pwessel/gshhg/
https://github.com/GenericMappingTools/gshhg-gmt/releases/tag/2.3.7
Retrieved gshhg-shp-2.3.7.zip. High-resolution GSHHS levels 1–5 selected.
LGPL v3 or later. Full selected input geometry, original data license and the
LGPL/GPL notices accompany the source. No GSHHG political border layer is used.
Derived map IDs retain the game scenario's political data.

Natural Earth admin-1, map-unit, disputed-area, lakes and city reference data
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
Public domain. Data derived from Natural Earth vector repository. Disputed-area
outlines are geographic reference, not automatic modern control assignments.

Myanmar township geometry: geoBoundaries gbOpen MMR ADM3
Boundary sources: The Myanmar Analytics Project, USAID.
https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/MMR/ADM3/
CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The full metadata is bundled as tools/cartography/myanmar_source.json. Township
polygons are merged as a geographic proxy for Northern Wa's core; no 2008 legal
status or 2024 Hopang transfer is assigned to January 2000.

Control proxies
Upper Kodori, Akhalgori and Georgian village corridors before the 2008 war use
explicit approximate polygons. They are not measured ceasefire-line data.
Sources and coordinates: tools/cartography/control_2000_overrides.geojson.
UNOMIG background; Secretary-General report S/2000/39; OSCE/ODIHR 2008 report.
The southern Wa footprint is likewise an approximate scenario control polygon,
constrained to Burmese land. It is not a whole administrative township.

Province cells
Deterministic gameplay cells clipped inside native Natural Earth regions, using
fresh IDs for the 0.1.6 layout. These are newly generated gameplay
geometry, not real-world administrative subdivisions or exact HOI4 outlines.
