Source metadata and full GeoJSON are bundled in tools/cartography/vatican_reference_022r2*; the derived simplified polygon preserves the source's geographic scale. geoBoundaries database attribution: William & Mary geoLab / geoBoundaries (Runfola et al.).

This is an approximate geographic reference, not an exact legal/cadastral border. Its measured polygon area differs from the official stated territorial area. The province area label uses the Vatican State's stated 0.44 km²:
https://vaticanstate.va/en/state-and-government/general-informations/geography.html

Game colouring, ray picking and border drawing share the same derived polygon; the coarse atlas identity pixel does not enlarge the visible or clickable country. No new country or territorial claim is added.
