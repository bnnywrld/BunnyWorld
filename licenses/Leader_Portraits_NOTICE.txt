BunnyWorld leader portraits

Original DDS graphics by the Millennium Dawn development team and contributors,
converted without cropping/resizing from the official Millennium Dawn repository:
https://github.com/MillenniumDawn/Millennium-Dawn
Pinned source revision: e231ce3935a558f1d7baaa3c9bf15ef888cd3f04

The upstream repository declares Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International. The full license is retained in Millennium_Dawn_LICENSE.txt.
Original upstream paths, Git blob IDs and source/output SHA-256 hashes
are recorded in data/leader_portraits.json. Original DDS bytes are cached outside the checkout. PNG assets losslessly encode
the decoded pixels, without resampling. UI display scales images to fit its panels.

Reproduction: python tools/import_leader_portraits.py
Starting leader names and office assignments come from the user's MD scenario
archive; the pinned upstream image source supplies only matching graphics.
No endorsement of BunnyWorld by the original authors or depicted people is implied.
