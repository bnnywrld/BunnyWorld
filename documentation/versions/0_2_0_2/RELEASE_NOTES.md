# BunnyWorld 0.2.0.2

- Replaced geometrical province cells with actual Millennium Dawn province shapes registered to the globe's accepted State geometry. Final count: 13,204 land provinces, compared with 13,221 usable reference land definitions. Seventeen tiny definitions do not survive registration; no lake/sea definitions are imported as land provinces. Original source IDs are recorded for traceability. Coastlines, borders, polar areas and small islands use controlled adaptations rather than wrapping the flat bitmap unchanged.
- Retained every accepted country and State identity, owner, core, claim, building value and population total. Province identities are rebuilt for this fresh scenario. Cities, metropolitan groups, district names and urban/rural population accounting follow the new layout. All 1,735 represented settlements remain linked.
- Added six native 4096-square surface photographs and a new full-frame forest canopy master. Eight compressed, mipmapped material layers remain resident. Balanced/High colour layers occupy 89,478,724 bytes; Light layers occupy 22,369,860 bytes. These figures are material bytes, not total memory. Forest imagery is a generated 1254-square master prepared at 4096; the new photographic materials have native 4K detail. No numeric “5x quality” guarantee is implied.
- Raised mountain exaggeration from 4 to 8 while retaining the gentle approximately 80-metre land lift and smooth coastal taper. GPU displacement, CPU picking, rivers, borders and scenery share the same height field.
- Physical shore coverage now uses an independent signed-distance mask with filtered edge coverage. Political selection retains lossless province IDs. Existing small islands, lake exclusion and canal/strait overrides remain. This smooths edge coverage; it does not invent missing coastline detail.
- Restored Antarctica's physical ice, exposed-terrain shading and elevation without assigning it invented political ownership or populated provinces.
- Added biome-limited arid colour variation in western North America, central/western Asia and interior Australia. Dry grass/scrub differs from wet grassland; forest categories and wetter southern Mexico retain their vegetation. This is a regional rendering adjustment, not a new aridity simulation.
- Expanded scenery from 55,296 to 117,367 buildings across 2,237 cached clusters and 1,446 city/metropolitan groups. Street blocks, avenues and empty plazas spread skylines over a wider area. Displayed-footprint checks reject building overlaps, reserve monument grounds and clear mapped river courses. Three-times height styling remains; landmark horizontal proportions are reduced to 60% of their previous enlarged width. Nearby caps are 2,048 buildings and 96 clusters.
- Increased named rivers from 83 to 350, including another tier of important tributaries. Rivers gain a larger screen width at close zoom; minor tributaries fade sooner when zooming out. Suez, Panama, Bosporus and Dardanelles remain reciprocal links; the Black Sea reaches the world ocean.
- Preserved fixed terrain texture handles, the one reusable 27,985-vertex close-up grid, startup loading progress, camera tilt, scenery fade/toggle, city label priority, compact information UI and the four simultaneous demographic charts.

Population/building growth, naval access/control simulation and old-save migration are outside this patch. Source-map registration, population estimates and representative scenery are approximate at game scale. This remains a private geopolitical prototype.
