- Two Pearl River Delta fragments owned by CHI have 0 population but 11 and 25 buildings, which suggests buildings were attached to the wrong state.
- Many small populations look unrealistic (for example Clipperton Island 579, Monaco 4,070), likely retained scenario totals.
