# Small-State changes in 0.2.0.17

All 97 input rows have a disposition below. Population totals are retained; demographic and economic refinement is deferred. Scenario ownership follows de facto administration in 2000, rather than duplicate States for opposing claims.

Two unoccupied disputes have no exclusive de facto controller to assign: Hans Island and Perejil retain their previous scenario assignment, with explicit control_2000_note metadata. These are conventions, not findings of sovereignty.

The former Jamaican Serranilla footprint is actually Bajo Nuevo by coordinates. The previous Colombian Bajo Nuevo footprint is Serranilla. Both now use Colombian administration. Mainland land immediately outside Melilla remains Moroccan; it is merged into Oriental rather than annexed to Spain.

Monaco, Nauru and Palau use their own existing country tags. Timor-Leste uses TIM with UNTAET administration metadata for the 2000 start. Their current demographic/economic values are inherited placeholders pending the planned data update.
