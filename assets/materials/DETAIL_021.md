# 0.1.11 surface-detail material

Created 14 October 2026 with the built-in Image Generation tool; a new production material atlas, not a replacement geographic texture.

Master: assets/materials/detail_master_021.png. Runtime atlas: assets/materials/surface_detail.png. Families in reading order: temperate woodland, sandy desert, grassland/countryside, mountain rock. Geography, biome blending, elevation and snow placement remain determined by the existing terrain imagery and NOAA elevation data.

Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production diffuse surface-material atlas for a semi-realistic 3D Earth geopolitical game. Generate one square high-resolution 2048 by 2048 image divided into exactly FOUR equal square texture tiles in a 2 by 2 layout with NO gaps, borders, labels, text or symbols. TOP LEFT: natural temperate woodland canopy with small irregular trees, earthy muted green, convincing fine organic variation. TOP RIGHT: sandy desert with fine dunes, wind-carved ripples and sparse gravel patches, warm muted ochre and beige. BOTTOM LEFT: natural grassland/countryside with fine grasses, muted olive green, tiny irregular soil and field variations, no huge geometric fields. BOTTOM RIGHT: mountain rock terrain, fine fractured stones, subdued slate gray and brown, light grit and crevices, no snow baked into it. Every tile is a perfectly flat orthographic TOP-DOWN material sample, evenly lit diffuse ALBEDO with no directional shadows, no perspective, no horizon, no geographic coastline, no map layout, no objects, no buildings. Each square's opposite edges should tile seamlessly on its own. Fine dense believable surface detail, restrained cinematic realism, sharp production texture, cohesive color grading. The purpose is detail added over real geographic imagery, not a replacement map.
